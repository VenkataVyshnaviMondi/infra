# Kubera Infra

Private workspace for an Indian property and furniture business. Staff work from shared inventory and assigned customer records to record enquiries, visits, negotiations, follow-ups, bookings, advance receipts and completed transactions.

Two roles only: Admin and Employee. Admin owns access, assignment and transaction completion. Employees manage their listings and assigned customers. Customers have no accounts. No public marketplace or registration.

Categories: Plots (Lease, Sale); Flats (Rent, Sale); Individual Houses (Rent, Sale); Furniture (Sofa, Dining, Beds, Wardrobes, Home Decor; always Sale). Transaction type and furniture subtype are separate database fields.

Availability, per-listing customer interest and lead stage are independent concepts. Conversions require completed transactions. Counts derive from saved interactions. History and employee attribution survive reassignment, cancellation and reversal.

Approved identity: supplied Kubera Infra logo, navy, restrained gold and white. Desktop work tables and mobile employee workflows. INR, sqft and Asia/Kolkata throughout.
